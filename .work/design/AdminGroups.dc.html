Naming a leader records a fact, not a permission
Everywhere else in this canvas, making someone a leader hands them an app: attendance, RSVP, the hosting rota, and the prompt on M23.

None of that exists yet, so this field says who leads and grants nothing. The permission arrives with the app, and W9 already describes what a handover has to do on the day it does.
Harapan has no leader, and that is allowed
A group with no leader is a real state in a church, not a validation error. It is also the most useful thing this screen can show an office: five groups, one of them quietly unled since the coordinator moved away.
