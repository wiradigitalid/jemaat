This computer is shared
Signs out when the browser closes. Leave it ticked in a church office.
No number on file? Only another administrator can add one — there is nothing to self-serve here, by design.
The hole this closes
Named at iteration 16 — the office cannot sign in to the web at all — and still sitting open on S1 twenty-six iterations later, because it was cheap and nothing depended on it.

Web first makes it the front door for everybody: the office, the leader and the member all arrive through this screen. It stops being cheap and starts being first.
What a link cannot do
It expires in ten minutes and once used it is dead, so a forwarded message is not an account.

It carries no role. Whatever W13 granted is what the person gets, and a link handed to the wrong volunteer grants nothing they did not already have.
Two administrators, still the rule
W13 refuses a church with one. It matters more here: the only way back in is a phone number in the register, so a church with one administrator and one lost phone is locked out of its own roll.
The dependency this makes unavoidable
A WhatsApp sender was already the second external dependency in R0 (S7). Now it is also how anyone reaches the office at all. The 6-digit fallback exists so a vendor outage is a slow morning rather than a locked building.
