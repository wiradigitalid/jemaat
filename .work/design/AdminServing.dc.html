Departments, roles, and what a person is interested in
Areas of involvement was a flat list of five words typed into a person. It becomes master data: a department holds serving roles, and a person expresses interest in a role. Two levels, no third — and the department is a heading in the picker, never a thing you can choose.

Seven rules that keep it from rotting
1
A role belongs to exactly one department
Moving it is allowed and recorded. A role that floats between departments makes every count ambiguous.
2
The catalogue is per church
A congregation with no multimedia team never sees those roles. Every church starts from a seed list and edits it down.
3
Deactivate, never delete
People have already expressed interest, and one day a roster will point at it. A dead role stops being offered and keeps resolving.
4
Search matches both levels, only one is choosable
Typing a department name surfaces its roles; the heading itself stays a label. Nobody is ever interested in Music, they are interested in Keyboardist.
5
A chosen role always carries its department
Coordinator exists in four departments. The chip reads Music / Coordinator or it is worthless in a list.
6
Interest is a wish, not a roster
It says someone is willing. It never means they are scheduled, and nothing in this release schedules anybody.
7
Interest is per church, like standing
Willing to sing here says nothing about the church someone attends on the other side of town.
The line this must not cross
Iteration 5 named serving as its own axis: a person belongs to teams, teams own slots, slots have dates. That is M10, M12, M13 and W3, and S1 cut all four to Later — the volunteer rotation a church actually asked for lives inside a care group.

This release adds the catalogue and the wish, and no schedule. The distance between the two is one table, and building it early would quietly restore the biggest cut on the release map.

What the office gets out of it
A list per role, which is the first thing a coordinator asks for: eighteen people said Singer and nobody has called them. W27 shows that list beside the role and lets it be exported, which is as far as this release goes towards a rota.
The seed list
Music, Multimedia, Prayer, Teaching, Visitation, Hospitality — twenty-four roles between them. A new church deletes what it does not have rather than inventing from an empty screen, the same argument W11 makes about the register itself.
