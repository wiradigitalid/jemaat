The shape under the screens
Not a schema — no types, no indexes, no audit columns. These are the seven expensive decisions written as structure, because prose in a sticky note does not survive week one of development, and each of them costs a migration afterwards.
People, and where they stand
person GLOBAL
id, full_name, date_of_birth, phone, phone_visibility
One row per human, ever. NOT per church - this is what lets someone belong to Surabaya and Bandung at once.
church PER CHURCH
id, code, name, city, worship_day, tier_labels
worship_day is a setting. Saturday here, Sunday elsewhere, and no screen hard-codes either.
membership JOIN
person_id, church_id, standing, lifecycle, since
standing: guest | community | registered. lifecycle: active | inactive | transferred | deceased. NO ROW AT ALL = not on our roll, which is how a guest speaker exists.
household PER CHURCH
id, church_id, name, address, area
household_member JOIN
person_id, household_id, role
role: head | spouse | child | other. Four, and no plans for a fifth.
What happens, and when
service PER CHURCH
id, church_id, starts_at, location
AN ENTITY, not a date. Two to four a week above 400 members, and everything below points at one.
service_content PER CHURCH
service_id, speaker_person_id, series, part, poster
speaker_person_id is nullable and has no FK to membership. That is the guest speaker.
care_group PER CHURCH
id, church_id, name, area, weekday, time, leader_person_id
care_group_member JOIN
person_id, care_group_id, since
Its own relation. Joining a group never touches membership, which is the whole promise made to Community Members.
meeting PER CHURCH
id, care_group_id, starts_at, host_household_id
rsvp / attendance JOIN
person_id, meeting_id, answer | came, marked_by
Two tables, not one. What someone SAID and what actually happened are different facts, and M26 is built on the gap.
Cheap today, a migration tomorrow
1
A service is a thing, not a date
service.id — attendance, assignment, announcement all FK to it
2
Standing belongs to the pair, not the person
membership(person, church) — never person.standing
3
Lifecycle is its own column
membership.lifecycle ≠ membership.standing
4
Group membership is a relation
care_group_member — never person.care_group_id
5
Assignments point at a person
assignment.person_id, no FK to membership
6
Address on the household, standing on the person
household.address · membership.standing
7
One global table, everything else scoped
person is global · church_id on all the rest
Decision 7 is the one that looks like over-engineering on day one, when there is a single pilot church. It is also the only one that cannot be retrofitted — every other table can gain a column later, but making person global after it was per-church means rewriting every row and every join.
Deliberately absent, and each absence is a decision made on a screen: no attendance_rate, no engagement_score, no last_seen — M23 refuses a count, and a column that exists will eventually be shown. No dismissed_prompts table — M23 promised nothing is recorded, and W9 pays that promise when a leader hands over. No person.church_id — that single field would quietly undo decisions 2 and 7 together.
