Build in this order
W11 already argued the principle: a group that works beats a list that is finished.
1
The church record and tenancy
One row before any person exists. The zone is set here or every timestamp is a guess
2
Admin auth, two administrators
The vendor template has a lead time. Start it in week one
3
Households, then people
The household holds the address, so it is the first table with a shape
4
The map, last of the register
Search and a draggable pin. Everything works without it, worse
5
Care groups, then the serving catalogue
Both cheap. The catalogue ships empty, so W29 is what the office meets and the interest field waits on it
6
Import, then merge
Two weeks together. Never ship the first without the second
7
Export
Before the second church signs, not the first
What the mobile build inherits
Every M, O and P artboard stays valid against the same API. The app is not a second product — it is the other three roles arriving at a register that already holds real data, which is the easier order to build in and the harder one to sell.
Closed by this release
The office could not sign in. Named at iteration 16, open for twenty-six. W18 and W19.
Time zones were named and never applied. Every screen said 19.30 and none said which. W25 holds it now.
Creating a person was mobile-only. W2 had an Add button pointing at an app screen. W22 and W23.
Care groups could only arrive by import. A church starting one in month two had nowhere to do it. W24.
Household did two jobs under one word. S14 splits the address from the kinship, and W26 draws it.
Involvement was five words typed into a person. It is master data now: a department, its roles, and an interest that points at one. S15 and W27.
Still open, and still not mine
No DEC- exists for anything, and the scope decision on this page is now the largest of them.
The tier names changed to Guest, Member and Registered Member. Older sheets still say Community Member in prose.
S5 predates the eleven-field person and the household split. It is the next sheet to re-derive.
