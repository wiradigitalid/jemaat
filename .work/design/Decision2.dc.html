The noticing prompt
The warta question was about cost, so a member’s week could settle it. This one cannot. The same screen is pastoral care in one church and surveillance in another, and the difference is not in the pixels — so the only honest place to start is what the design can actually hold, and what it cannot.
The design enforces this
It reaches one leader. There is no list view, anywhere, for anyone.
No count, no percentage, no score - not on this screen and not in the data model behind it.
No status changes automatically. Only the office marks anyone Inactive, after talking to them.
Dismissing it costs nothing and records nothing, and it does not travel to the next leader (W9).
The design cannot
Stop a pastor asking leaders to forward the names. No product can police a WhatsApp message.
Decide why attendance is being taken. The same tick means care in one church and control in another.
Make a leader treat it as a nudge rather than a fact, though the screen says so plainly.
Tell the member it happened. M27 shows them their attendance, but never that someone was prompted.
A Cut it
M23 is standalone. Nothing else in the product depends on it.
Attendance is still collected — M26 needs it, and so does the group’s own history.
The leader still sees who said yes and did not come, after every single meeting.
A leader of fourteen people already knows who has been missing. The pattern detection is only genuinely needed at forty, or by an office watching two thousand.
Costs almost nothing at pilot size. The real loss only appears at scale.
B Ship it on
As drawn, for every church, from day one.
The pastoral catch is automated, and quiet drift gets noticed earlier than a human would notice it.
Every church inherits four conditions it never agreed to hold.
A church that turns it into a report has broken a promise this product printed on the screen - and the product handed them the material.
Defensible, and the fastest way to be wrong in a church that was never asked.
C Off until asked for
The capability ships. The assumption never does.
Default off. A church office turns it on, and the four unenforceable conditions are shown at that moment - not buried in a policy.
Turning it on is a decision with a name and a date against it, like every other consequential act in this product (W6, W9, W10).
A church that has never thought about it is never handed it.
Recommended. Ship the capability, never the assumption.
