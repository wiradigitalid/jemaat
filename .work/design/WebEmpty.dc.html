One thing is worth knowing before you type anything. A household holds the address. A person holds their standing. So change an address once and the whole family moves; change someone from Community to Registered and only they change.

How households work
Bulk import from a spreadsheet arrives in the next release. Until then this is typing — which is why the order on the right matters more than the total.
An hour, in this order
The register does not have to be complete to be useful. It has to be useful before it is complete, or nobody finishes it.
1
Your own household
Four people, five minutes. Now you have seen every screen a member will see.
2
The care group leaders
Six or eight people. They are the ones who will fill in the rest for you.
3
One care group
Give it a leader and a night. It can meet this week with three members in it.
4
Everyone else, slowly
Or wait for the import next release. A group that works beats a list that is finished.
After step 3 the app is genuinely running: that group can RSVP, take attendance and claim a host, with eleven people still untyped.
